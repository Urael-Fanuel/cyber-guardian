Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect the minimum data needed to provide the Service:

  • Code you submit for scanning — sent to Anthropic's API, then discarded
  • Email address (only if you subscribe to alerts)
  • IP address — for rate limiting only, not stored long-term
  • Payment info — if you upgrade to a paid plan, handled entirely by Stripe

2. What We DON'T Collect

✓ We do NOT store the code you scan. It is sent to Anthropic, analyzed, and then discarded. We keep only a hash (one-way) for 1 hour to enable caching of identical scans.

✓ We do NOT track you across the web. No third-party advertising trackers.

✓ We do NOT sell your data. Period.

3. How We Use Data

  • Code: Sent to Anthropic Claude API for analysis, then deleted
  • Email: Used only to send the alerts you signed up for. Unsubscribe anytime.
  • IP: Used to prevent abuse (rate limiting). Not linked to your identity.

4. Third Parties

The Service uses these providers, all bound by their own privacy policies:

  • Anthropic — for AI-powered code analysis. Their policy: anthropic.com/privacy
  • Netlify — for hosting and request processing
  • Stripe — for payment processing (only if you upgrade)

5. Data Retention

  • Scan code: deleted immediately after analysis
  • Scan hashes (cache): 1 hour
  • Subscriber emails: until you unsubscribe
  • Payment records: 7 years (legal requirement)

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability

Email privacy@your-domain.com to exercise these rights.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • cg-lang: Remembers your language preference
  • cg-quota-YYYY-MM: Tracks your free quota

These are stored locally in your browser — no tracking cookies.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the update here with a new date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@your-domain.com
